Unapotumia **Binance P2P**, ni muhimu kuchagua merchant kwa umakini kabla ya kufanya transaction.

Angalia mambo haya muhimu:

* **Completion Rate**

   — Chagua merchant mwenye transaction history nzuri.
* **Number of Orders**

   — Angalia idadi ya transactions alizokamilisha.
* **Price**

   — Linganisha bei na merchants wengine.
* **Payment Method**

   — Hakikisha payment method inakufaa.
* **Terms**

   — Soma masharti ya advertisement kabla ya kuendelea.

### Muhimu

Usikubali kushinikizwa kufanya transaction nje ya Binance. Kama unauza crypto, **thibitisha payment imeingia kwenye account yako kabla ya ku-release crypto.**

**Chagua merchant kwa umakini, verify kila kitu, kisha fanya transaction.**
